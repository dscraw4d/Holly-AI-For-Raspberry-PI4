HOLLY AI LEARNING OS v0.49.29 - American male Queeg voice

INSTALL
Power off the Pi and back up kernel8.img on the SD boot partition. Replace ONLY
kernel8.img with the one in this ZIP. Preserve config.txt, firmware, partitions
and all data. Boot, then press Ctrl+F5 in Chrome and select New chat.
Run version over SSH to confirm v0.49.29. Login remains Rimmer / smeghead.
Do not reflash or format. HDMI-disabled kernel-recovery.img is optional recovery;
rename it kernel8.img only if needed.

QUEEG'S VOICE
Mention QUEEG in web/voice chat. Queeg now selects an English US male voice,
preferring Google US English Male if that exact voice is available, then known
US male voices such as Microsoft David, Mark, Guy or Christopher. His slightly
lower pitch and stern persona remain. The generic Google US English voice is
not assumed to be male, and female or British voices are not substituted.
If no identified US male voice is present, captions continue and a notice asks
you to enable an English US male voice such as Microsoft David, then refresh.
Voice availability comes from Chrome and the device's installed speech voices.
This is speech synthesis, not the actor's original voice.
